Reference code: renderer/FrameQueue.cpp, renderer/VulkanSurfacePresenter.cpp, renderer/VulkanOutput.cpp, MelonInstance.cpp and Android input/render coordination. These implement a melonDS-specific Vulkan rendering and presentation pipeline. This custom SeedlessDS build instead adds a CPU-frame Vulkan display backend, with bounded GPU submissions, late frame capture and compatibility fallback. It does not port melonDS hardware 3D emulation or claim equivalent latency measurements.

Vulkan synchronization and pre-rotation also follow primary Khronos and Android documentation. Full design, modifications, validation scope and source URLs are in VULKAN_LOW_LATENCY.md in the accompanying modified source archive. Vulkan shaders and their generated SPIR-V header are included.
